A River Set Free

Environment Agency · Natural England · Tweed Forum

Restoring the River Breamish. Bringing together national resilience strategy with on-the-ground partnership action, with 2.5 km of river restored.

Project details

The challenge

Part of the £5.4 million EU-funded LIFE Waders project, the restoration of the River Breamish on Northumberland’s Harehope Estate was 15 years in the making. The film had to show why, and how, a project costing nearly £3 million was justified economically, environmentally and socially, to three audiences at once:

  • The public: to build support for the policy and inform without lecturing.
  • Landowners: whose consent the work depends on, and who needed to trust the partners.
  • Private funders: as large schemes rely more on blended finance, to position Tweed Forum as a credible delivery partner for future funding.

What we did

Tommy brought more than 20 years in business and a multidisciplinary environmental degree to the brief: the experience to win the trust of every kind of stakeholder, and to turn complex science into a clear, watchable story.

We filmed the project, managed a team of skilled freelancers and guided the steering group through the story. Few creatives can lead both the big-picture strategy and the detail of great film-making. At Oxbow, we do both.

Our approach

“A river finds the clearest course. So do we.”

An oxbow lake forms when a river cuts through a bend and finds a shorter route. We do the same with complex environmental subjects: find the clear message, and keep the place it came from in view.

Plain, not preachy

We explain the science in everyday words and never lecture.

Evidence first

We lead with what was measured, where and when.

Human scale

We tell stories of real places and the people who care for them.

Honest hope

We show what works without hiding what does not.

How we work

Four steps from the field to the finished work

  1. 01

    Listen

    We start on site, with the people who manage the land and water.

  2. 02

    Find the course

    We turn the evidence into one clear message and a plan to reach people.

  3. 03

    Make

    We design, write and film, and test with real audiences as we go.

  4. 04

    Measure

    We track what changed and report it honestly, including what did not work.

Questions

Before you get in touch

How much does a project cost?

It depends on scope. After a first call we send a fixed-fee proposal, broken down by phase, so you can see exactly where the budget goes. Print, media and venue costs are quoted separately.

How long does a project take?

We agree the timeline in the proposal, phase by phase, and work back from any fixed dates such as a launch, a funding deadline or a season on site.

How do you measure results?

We agree the measures at the start, such as reach, views, cost per action or sign-ups, and track them through the project. You get a results report at the end, including what did not work.

Do you work with public-sector procurement?

Yes. Tell us which framework or process you use and we will shape the proposal to fit it.
